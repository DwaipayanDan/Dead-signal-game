Kita Island. 300 km from mainland Japan. Communications are dead. Your boat is gone. Rescue is expected in 15 days — if you can survive until then.

Mobile: use the left stick to move; FIRE shoots toward the screen centre; USE interacts. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, E to interact, Shift to sprint. For friends, run the included server and share its public URL + room code. This is a playable prototype, not a finished commercial game.
